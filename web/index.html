Methods, scope and audit files

What does the planner optimise?

A binary mixed-integer problem finds a minimum-cover seed for factor-level and observed-pair requirements. A rank-aware, regularised D-optimal-inspired heuristic adds informative settings up to a chosen budget. Each selected setting contributes multiple requirement observations. Greedy augmentation and sequencing are heuristics, not proofs of global campaign optimality.

What do the results establish?

The aggressive historical subset loses too much predictive information despite complete declared coverage. A separate development-only study shortlists retaining all settings with six repeats for independent validation. Neither 83.47% nor 40.50% is a proven future physical-test saving. Experimental requirements are illustrative factor coverage, not legal compliance or OEM acceptance criteria.

Where do the measurements come from?

UCI Condition monitoring of hydraulic systems, Helwig, Pignanelli and Schütze (2015), DOI 10.24432/C5CW21, CC BY 4.0. The dataset is a public hydraulic benchmark. There is no live hardware connection or proprietary manufacturer data.
